# 🍳 Foodgo — Merchant & Kitchen Portal Manual

The **Foodgo Merchant Portal** enables kitchen managers, restaurant owners, and store merchants to accept and prepare incoming orders, manage store-specific inventory, and view payout balances.

---

## 🚪 Accessing the Merchant Portal

- **URL**: `https://yourdomain.com/merchant.html` (or `https://yourdomain.com/?portal=merchant`)

---

## ⚡ Core Merchant Capabilities

1. **Kitchen Order Feed**:
   - Audio chime notifications on new incoming customer orders.
   - Print preparation tickets / KOT with item customizations, toppings, and notes.
   - Update preparation status to `Preparing` or `Ready for Pickup`.

2. **Menu & Inventory Control**:
   - Mark items `In Stock` or `Sold Out` in real time.
   - Modify product prices and preparation times.

3. **Store Profile & Timings**:
   - Toggle store open/closed state.
   - Configure minimum order requirements.

4. **Merchant Wallet & Payouts**:
   - Track total net earnings after platform commission.
   - Request withdrawals and view historical settlement logs.
