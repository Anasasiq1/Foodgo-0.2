# 🛵 Foodgo — Delivery Partner Portal Manual

The **Foodgo Delivery Portal** provides delivery riders and fleet drivers with a mobile-optimized interface for managing order assignments, navigation routing, customer drop-offs, and daily delivery earnings.

---

## 🚪 Accessing the Delivery Portal

- **URL**: `https://yourdomain.com/delivery.html` (or `https://yourdomain.com/?portal=delivery`)

---

## ⚡ Rider Workflow

1. **Shift Status Toggle**:
   - Switch between **Online (Ready for Orders)** and **Offline**.

2. **Order Assignment Notification**:
   - View pickup location, restaurant contact details, customer delivery address, and expected drop-off time.
   - Accept or decline assigned delivery runs.

3. **In-Transit Status Updates**:
   - Step 1: `Arrived at Kitchen`
   - Step 2: `Order Picked Up` (Changes order status to *In Transit*)
   - Step 3: `Delivered to Customer` (Prompts for OTP / Cash Collection confirmation)

4. **Earnings & Tips**:
   - View per-order delivery fee earnings and customer tips.
   - Weekly payout summaries.
